# CarDaddy Legal Copy Drafts

These are product-copy drafts for attorney review. They are not legal advice and must not be treated as final agreements.

## Customer Platform Notice

CarDaddy is a connection platform that collects service requests and may share an authorized request with a compatible independent provider. The provider is not presented as a CarDaddy employee. The provider determines diagnosis, availability, pricing, payment methods, repair terms, and any warranty directly with the customer. The customer pays the provider directly unless a future written checkout process clearly states otherwise.

## Consent to Share Information

I authorize CarDaddy to share the information in this request, including my contact and approximate location information, with a selected independent provider for the purpose of evaluating, accepting, scheduling, and communicating about this service opportunity.

## Independent Provider Acknowledgement

The applicant is seeking participation as an independent provider. The provider supplies their own tools and transportation, controls availability and service area, chooses which opportunities to accept, communicates and contracts directly with customers, sets prices, collects payment, and remains responsible for work, representations, licensing, insurance, and warranty obligations that apply to the provider.

## Private Media Notice

Application photographs, documents, and demonstration videos are collected for private evaluation. They are not automatically published or used in advertising. Any public or promotional use requires a separate, specific authorization.

## Complaint Procedure Draft

Customers may submit a private report after verifying case information. CarDaddy may request evidence and responses from the parties, document the review, and take internal network action. Submission does not guarantee a particular result and does not replace emergency services, law enforcement, insurance claims, or legal remedies.

## Suspension and Appeal Draft

Providers are not penalized for honestly declining an opportunity. Verified failures after acceptance, misuse of information, fraud, theft, threats, or serious safety issues may result in warning, reduced priority, investigation, temporary suspension, or ineligibility. A provider should receive notice and an opportunity to respond and provide evidence, except when immediate preventive suspension is reasonably required for safety or fraud review.

## Cancellation, Ratings, and Warranty Draft Topics

- Define when customers and providers may cancel and what must be communicated.
- Explain how ratings are collected, challenged, moderated, retained, and displayed.
- Confirm that repair and warranty terms are agreed directly with the independent provider.
- Avoid promises of guaranteed response, guaranteed provider availability, guaranteed repairs, work volume, or income.

## State Counsel Checklist

Review in Mississippi first, then Louisiana and Alabama: marketplace/operator classification, independent-contractor law, automotive repair and estimate rules, towing requirements, sales tax and licensing, commercial insurance, privacy and breach notice law, electronic signatures, TCPA/email consent, consumer protection, background checks, dispute handling, ratings, record retention, membership terms, automatic renewal, and payment processing.
